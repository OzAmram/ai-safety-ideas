Open problems in AI safety

Research ideas that working researchers would like to see done. Pick one up, and if the poster has highlighted it, they'll help you along.

Post an idea
  1. Pick an idea and open it on GitHub.
  2. Comment /claim to say you're working on it, or /claim solo if you'd rather not take collaborators. Most ideas allow several people at once; you'll see who else is on it.
  3. Renew every 30 days with /renew plus one line on progress. If you don't reply to the reminder within a week, the claim is released.
  4. Finish with /done and a link to your write-up, or /release if you stop. Both help the next person.
